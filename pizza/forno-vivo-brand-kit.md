# Client Brand & Design Specification — FORNO VIVO

## 1. Client Overview
* **Brand Name:** Forno Vivo (Wood-Fired Artisan Pizza)
* **Location & Market:** Madinet Qaft / Upper Egypt & Delivery Zones (Currency: EGP)
* **Target Audience:** Families, students, and late-night food lovers looking for fast, hot, authentic wood-fired pizza.
* **Brand Vibe:** Warm, bold, appetizing, fast, and artisan—like a glowing brick oven at night.

## 3. Typography & Font Pairing

### English Typography
* **Display & Headings:** `Fraunces` (Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800) — Gives an authentic, warm, artisanal pizzeria feel.
  * *Fallback:* `Georgia, serif`
* **Body, UI & Pricing:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700) — Clean, modern, and super readable for menus, toppings, and mobile checkout.
  * *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`

### Arabic Typography (Optional Bilingual Support)
* **Headings:** `Cairo` (Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800)
* **Body & Menu Items:** `Tajawal` (Regular 400 / Medium 500 / Bold 700)

### Type Scale
* **Hero Headline (H1):** `56px` Desktop / `36px` Mobile (Line-height: `1.1`)
* **Section Titles (H2):** `36px` Desktop / `26px` Mobile (Line-height: `1.2`)
* **Card Titles (H3):** `20px` (Line-height: `1.3`)
* **Body & Ingredients:** `15px` (Line-height: `1.5`)
* **Buttons & Badges:** `14px` SemiBold / Bold (Letter-spacing: `0.02em`)

---

## 4. Client Functional Requirements
1. **Fast Ordering Path:** Users must be able to toggle **Delivery vs. Pickup** right from the sticky top bar.
2. **Visual Impact:** Every pizza card must showcase overhead transparent `.png` photography with soft shadows so the crust and melted cheese pop against `#120E0C`.
3. **Clear Pricing & Sizes:** Show **Small (S)**, **Medium (M)**, and **Large (L)** toggles directly on each card with instant price updates.
4. **Custom Pizza Builder:** Include a dedicated interactive section where customers pick crust, sauce, and extra toppings.
5. **Trust & Urgency:** Feature a **30-Minute Hot Delivery** guarantee and a **Buy 1 Get 1** hero promo banner.
